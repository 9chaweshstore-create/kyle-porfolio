About Me

Hey, I'm Kyle,

I've spent the last few years obsessing over what makes people stop scrolling and click one frame, one expression, one contrast choice at a time. Thumbnail design isn't decoration to me; it's the first impression your video ever makes.

I work with creators who take their channel seriously, turning raw footage and rough ideas into thumbnails that convert. No templates, no guesswork every design is built around your content and your audience.
